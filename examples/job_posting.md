# Example Job Posting

## Role

Senior Technical Program Manager, AI Platform

## Responsibilities

- Lead cross-functional programs across product, engineering, data science, and operations.
- Create execution plans, identify risks, and communicate progress to executives.
- Drive adoption of internal AI platform capabilities.
- Define success metrics and improve delivery processes.

## Qualifications

- 7+ years in program management, product operations, or technical leadership.
- Experience with platform products, AI/ML, data products, or developer tools.
- Strong written communication and stakeholder management.
- Track record delivering ambiguous, cross-functional initiatives.
